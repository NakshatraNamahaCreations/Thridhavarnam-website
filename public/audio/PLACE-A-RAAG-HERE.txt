Drop a traditional Indian instrumental track here as `raag-ambient.mp3` and
the splash screen's "Enter with Sound" button will play it on loop.

Suggested tracks (royalty-free / Creative Commons):

  - Sitar ambient / drone on freemusicarchive.org
  - "Raag" search on pixabay.com/music — many CC0 sitar pieces
  - YouTube Audio Library — search "Indian", "sitar", "tabla"

Naming:   raag-ambient.mp3
Length:   ~3 to 6 minutes (it loops anyway)
Volume:   The player fades it in at 0.35 — mix accordingly

If the file is missing, the site degrades gracefully — the gate still
appears, but the toggle silently does nothing.
